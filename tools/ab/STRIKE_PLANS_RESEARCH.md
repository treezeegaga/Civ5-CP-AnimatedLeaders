# Early strike plans for Civ V BNW warmonger civs (human play research)

**Scope.** Most sources are CivFanatics forum threads from 2012 to 2018. Most are BNW at Deity or Immortal, Standard pace, Standard map size.

**Turns.** Guide turns are kept at the pace they were given (usually Standard). Quick-pace turn ≈ Standard turn x 0.67 [I]. For example, Standard T60-70 ≈ Quick T40-47, which fits the user's Aztec example (declared T41-50 Quick, took a capital at T54 Quick).

**Labels.**
- **[S]** means the claim comes from the linked source, either quoted or closely paraphrased.
- **[I]** means it is my inference or my own game-rules knowledge. Unit stats marked [I] are from memory of BNW and should be checked against the CP unit tables.

**Excluded sources.** Three kinds of source were left out:
- Vox Populi / CBO threads, such as "The Huns Guide - Early Warfare" (2019) and the 2018 "Japan Strategy Help" thread. VP changes the uniques, so they do not apply to plain CP.
- Civ4-era threads.
- Carl's Guides and the Civ fandom wiki. Neither host was reachable from this session.

## 0. General patterns (these apply to every civ below)

**G1. The standard non-UU strike is the "Archer to Composite Bowman" timing attack.**
- Build Archers early and save gold. Beeline Construction after one luxury tech, then upgrade on the turn Construction lands.
- Luven: "As soon as you hit Construction, upgrade as many Archers as possible." [S1]
- Gold to reserve: Resipsa says keep "80gold for each archer to upgrade" [S1]. Tabarnak says keep about 70 x your unit count [S2].
- Army size: 5-7 CBs move in, then 2-3 more follow [S1]. Another poster says 4-5 CBs make "conquest pretty easy at around Turn 60" [S1]. A third says 7 CBs plus a Great General can take a 23-24 defense AI capital [S3].
- Timing at Standard pace: around T55-70 [S1][S2][S3].
- The user's Aztec run is this same pattern, with the Jaguars as the melee.

**G2. Melee is required to capture.**
- Ranged units cannot take a city. Bring at least 2 melee units, so that losing one does not stall the capture.
- Cromagnus: "Bring two melee units in case one dies during capture" [S4].
- Luven: "a scout AND a warrior. Or a spearman on a rough terrain tile" [S1].

**G3. Pick the target by terrain and walls, not only by distance.**
- Deau: "all you need is a good flatlands target" [S5].
- Luven: "much harder, sometimes impossible if your target is in rough terrain and the city is on a hill with walls" [S1].
- Erneiz Hyde: CBs are fine "until up to around 23 city strength", after which you need Crossbows [S1].
- Pilgrim: "You need cooperating terrain and victim" [S5].
- Rule for the AI [I]: abort or retarget if the target is on a hill, walled, and has more than about 20 strength at arrival. Also abort if no suitable target is within about 8-10 tiles.

**G4. Few cities, then conquer instead of expanding.**
- Cities before the strike: 2-3 (Tradition), or 3 with Liberty "if held to three cities" [S1][S2][S3].
- Attila's guide: "I'd much rather annex on t40 than plant a settler" [S4].

**G5. Pre-war harassment: steal workers, farm XP, buy allies.**
- Steal 1-2 workers before T20, "Optimally your war target" [S1]. Deau "DoWing between turns 5-20 to steal a worker and leave the war open" [S5].
- Hunt barbarians for XP, and with Honor for culture [S6][S7].
- Bribe a third civ into a war with the target first [S1][S4].

**G6. Diplomatic cost is high in BNW.**
- Several posters say early warmonger penalties last all game [S8][S9].
- Accepted because the conquest pays for itself, but it argues for 1 decisive target over many small wars [I].

**G7. Policy tree is contested.**
- Attila guide (Cromagnus): Honor [S4].
- CB-rush thread: Liberty is faster ("Liberty saves a ton of turns prior to t60"), and "Honor just isn't worth it" for a plain CB rush [S2].
- Honor wins when the civ has a culture- or faith-per-kill unique (Aztec, Celts) or many barbarians nearby [S6][S7][S10].

## 1. Huns (Attila)

**Strength [I + S4]**
- Horse Archer: replaces the Chariot Archer at **The Wheel**, needs no Horses, starts with Accuracy. "The strongest ranged unit you can build prior to Construction" [S4].
- Battering Ram: replaces the Spearman at **Bronze Working**. Attacks cities only, "40 strength vs cities" [S4].
- Trait: starts with Animal Husbandry, +production on pastures, razes cities at double speed.
- How long the spike lasts:
  - Horse Archers are "effective until t 150 at least" (budweiser, Immortal) [S11].
  - Rams fall off once cities get walls and promoted Pikes [I].
  - A Deity tier-list poster: they "can't finish off the world in the ancient/classical era" [S8].

**Type.** The **earliest striker** on the list. Cromagnus says the first army is ready "around t30", with a goal to "capture 1 capital every ten turns starting on t40" [S4]. Worst case: "start by t50" [S4]. All Standard pace.

**Opening (flexible)**
- Capital builds [S4]:
  - Scout first.
  - Monument second "on most maps."
  - Once The Wheel is in, Horse Archers continuously, with a Warrior or Ram as needed.
  - Older G&K advice is Scout, Monument, Worker (or steal one), Granary [S12].
- Research [S4]:
  1. The Wheel, or Mining first if the capital sits on a mined luxury.
  2. A luxury tech.
  3. Bronze Working ("only with spare happiness"), which unlocks the Ram.
  4. Horseback Riding.
  5. Then Writing, Math or Construction as needed.
- Grendeldef took Archery and The Wheel before Pottery so that Horse Archers could be upgraded early [S13].
- Policy: Honor (Military Tradition, then Professional Army). Tradition first only with strong capital tiles. Not Liberty: "There isn't time to build the Pyramids" [S4].
- Cities: 1-2 own cities, then annex conquests [S4][S11]. consentient: "Honor 2 city NC DomV", 5 captures by T87 at Immortal [S11].
- Before the war: steal workers; declare on a Military or Faith city-state if no civ has been met yet; Rams can take city-states early [S4][S12].

**Strike [S4][S11][S12]**
- Minimum force is "2 Horse Archers + 1 melee". Plan for 3 or more.
- A typical first capital: 5 Horse Archers + 1 Ram (consentient, Edinburgh) [S11].
- Moriarte: "Build/buy 2-3 Rams and 5 Horse Archers" [S12].
- No other siege is needed, since the Ram is the siege. Upgrades: none at the strike [I].
- Target: the closest neighbor. Avoid cities on hills; one Ram "can solo a city that is not on a hill" [S12].

**Exit conditions**
- Over-prepare rather than fail: "far better to over-estimate, and finish slower than it is to fail to capture" [S4].
- Great Wall:
  - Fire from range outside the wall and send melee in, or
  - take an easy nearby city first, then use roads [S4].
- City defense around 23 (Rome) was judged too strong for Horse Archers [S13].
- A strike stalls if it loses both melee units (budweiser) [S11]. AI rule [I]: if fewer than 2 melee or Rams are alive, pause and rebuild before attacking again.
- If Legions or Pikes defend on rough terrain, switch to a different, flatter target [S11][I].

## 2. Aztec (Montezuma)

**Strength [I]**
- Jaguar: replaces the Warrior, available at **no tech**, strength 8 vs 6.
  - Promotions: +33% and double movement in Forest/Jungle, heals on kill [S14].
  - The promotions stay after an upgrade [S14][S15].
- Trait: culture for each kill, doubled vs barbarians with the Honor opener [S10][S14].
- Floating Gardens: an economic building.
- How long the spike lasts:
  - The Jaguar is a T1 unit, good until Spearmen and Composite Bowmen are common, roughly Standard T50-70 [I].
  - Mixed with Archers, then CBs, it stretches to the CB window (G1). Rakshasa: "Aztecs unique attributes last way longer than the Huns" [S16].

**Type.** An early striker, but **not a Jaguar-only rush**. The proven pattern is Jaguars hunting barbarians, then a strike at the Archer/CB or Catapult timing.
- doveni (Immortal, Standard): 6 Jaguars + 2 Catapults captured a capital by T68 [S16].
- Gort: "The Huns are way better at the early rush than Aztecs are" [S16].

**Opening (flexible)**
- Builds:
  - 2-3 Jaguars first. They replace the Scout: "Jags = no need for culture ruins" (kb27787) [S10]. beetle: "My first build was Jaguar" [S17].
  - Then Granary, Library or Monument per the Deity Challenge players [S10][S14].
  - The user's run: Jaguar x3, Worker, Settler, Archer x4, Jaguar x2. This fits these sources [I].
- Policy: Honor opener. With Aztec kill culture it gives "3 policies ... by turn 25-30 on Standard speed" (Troll4Hire) [S14]. Then Tradition [S14].
- Research: a luxury tech, then Archery, then Construction (the CB timing), or Mathematics for Catapults (doveni) [S16][I].
- Cities: 2-3 [S16][I].
- Before the war: barbarian hunting in groups of 2-3 for XP and culture [S14]. Steal a worker early; Grendeldef "DoWed Attila for a Worker T7" [S17].

**Strike**
- About 6 Jaguars + 3-4 CBs (the user's run), or 6 Jaguars + 2 Catapults [S16].
- raider980 destroyed Bismarck "with just Archers and Jaguars" before the CB point at Deity [S17].
- Upgrades: Archers to CBs at Construction (G1). Jaguars upgrade to Swordsmen at Iron Working, and need Iron [S14].
- Siege: optional against an unwalled, flat or forest target. Bring 1-2 Catapults if the target has walls [S16][I].

**Exit conditions**
- Jaguars "die quickly, especially against cities" (FeiLing) [S14]. Thadian: losing a few Jaguars leaves too few healthy units [S16].
- AI rule [I]: abort if fewer than 4 healthy melee units remain, or if the target has walls and no siege is present.
- doveni's after-war trap: no gold for settlers, a lost happiness balance, then teching behind. Plan the economy after the first capital [S16].

## 3. Inca (Pachacuti) — POOR SOURCES

**Strength [I + S18]**
- Slinger: an Archer replacement at **Archery** with Withdraw Before Melee. The promotion stays after the upgrade to Composite Bowman [S18].
- Trait: Great Andean Road, ignore terrain cost on hills.
- Terrace Farm: an economic improvement, which the tier list rates as the main strength [S8].
- A forum poster called the Slinger "not really a make or break UU" [S18].

**Type.** Mostly an **economy-first** civ. No BNW CivFanatics thread describes an Inca rush.
- The Deity Challenge #5 (Inca) players all went Tradition/Liberty with Scouts and Settlers. Slingers were used only against barbarians [S19].
- Inferred plan [I]: a plain G1 Archer to CB timing attack, using the Slinger as the Archer. Withdraw Before Melee makes the ranged units safer. Hill movement lets the army reach and surround a hill-top city, which removes the usual "avoid hills" restriction (G3) for this civ only.

**Opening [I + S19]**
- Builds: Scout, Monument or Scout, Granary, Settler; Slingers from build 4-5 onward.
- Research: Pottery/Mining, a luxury tech, Archery, then Construction.
- Policy: Tradition.
- Cities: 2-3.
- Before the war: Slingers hunt barbarians, with clear tiles behind them so the withdrawal works [S18].

**Strike [I]**
- 5-7 CBs (upgraded Slingers) + 2 melee (Spearmen or Warriors).
- Declare around Standard T60-70.
- Hills are acceptable targets for the Inca. Walls without siege are not.

**Exit [I].** Same rules as G3. If no target is in reach by Construction + 10 turns, drop the war plan and go Tradition/economy. That is the Inca's real strength [S8].

## 4. Greece (Alexander)

**Strength [I + S20]**
- Hoplite: replaces the Spearman at **Bronze Working**, strength 13 vs 11 [I].
- Companion Cavalry (CC): replaces the Horseman at **Horseback Riding**, strength 14 with 5 moves, and generates Great Generals faster [I][S20].
- Trait: city-state influence decays slower; no unique building.
- Mounted units have a city-attack penalty, and CCs are "weak at taking cities" [S20 search summary].

**Type.** Contested.
- The BNW OP recommends building first (Tradition, Patronage) [S21].
- consentient: "I've had my fastest domination victories with Hoplites and CC" with Honor [S21].
- CraigMak: early war is a "steam roll" below Immortal. At Immortal/Deity, build infrastructure first, then "a good xbow rush" [S21].
- Tabarnak: a 2-3 city rush, "massive army around turn 35 or later at quick speed" [S21]. This is one of the few Quick-pace data points.

**Opening (flexible) [S21][I]**
- Builds:
  - Monuments first, then "chunk units", with no Granaries (Liberty, low production).
  - With high production: 2 cities with 2 Granaries (Tradition).
- Research: Bronze Working and Archery (defense and Archers), then Horseback Riding, then Construction.
- Policy: Honor if rushing; Tradition otherwise.
- Before the war: Hoplites take undefended city-states and earn city-state quest rewards [S20]. Do not keep captured city-states as Greece; the Greek trait favors allying them [S20].

**Strike [S20][I]**
- CCs or Horsemen to kill units in the field, CBs (G1) to drop city defense, and Hoplites to take the city.
- aguds: "If AI has lots of pikes, use hoplites. If not, use CC" [S20].
- Hoplites "need ranged support" [S20 search summary]. Declare around Standard T50-65 [I].

**Exit**
- Pike spam: CCs fail "short of Bismarck's Pike spam" [S20].
- A walled hill city without ranged support [I].
- Fallback: the city-state path (Patronage) [S21].

## 5. Celts (Boudicca)

**Strength [S7]**
- Pictish Warrior: replaces the Spearman at **Bronze Working**, strength 11, cost 56.
  - +20% fighting outside Celtic borders, pillage at no movement cost.
  - Gains faith equal to 50% of the defeated unit's strength on each kill.
- It "lacks the Spearman's bonus against mounted units" [S7].
- Upgrades to Pikeman and keeps the +20% but loses the faith-on-kill [S7][S22].
- Trait: faith from forests; Ceilidh Hall gives happiness.
- Spike: from Bronze Working, through Classical, then a second spike with upgraded Pikemen plus Crossbows plus Trebuchets in the Medieval era [S7].

**Type.** An early striker, or an early **harasser**.
- "Preemptive invasions of your neighbors is the Celtic way"; "an early Pictish Warrior/Composite Bow rush is something to be feared" [S7].
- TandooriChicken (G&K era): "six PWs are enough to capture non-capital AI cities even at deity"; 4-6 PWs + 2-3 Archers "beat Deity AIs"; "get going EARLY" [S3].
- Others say the Pictish Warrior is mainly for barbarian hunting or wars that do not take cities, because "BNW penalises early conquest" (FromBirth). VicRatlhead: pillage and steal workers, and the AI offers "extravagant tribute for peace" [S22].

**Opening (flexible) [S7][S22][I]**
- Research: Bronze Working early, because barbarians in BNW are numerous [S23]. Then Archery and Construction.
- Policy: Honor fits faith from kills [S7]. Many players prefer Tradition or Liberty [S22].
- Pantheon: God of War (faith from battles near a city) for war [S7], or God-King [S22].
- Builds: Scout, Monument, Pictish Warrior x2 for barbarians, Settler, then Pictish Warriors and Archers [I].
- Cities: 2-3.
- Before the war: barbarian hunting for faith, which secures the pantheon or religion [S22].

**Strike [S3][S7]**
- 4-6 Pictish Warriors + 2-3 Archers or CBs. Surround the target in one turn.
- The +20% bonus applies only outside Celtic land, so fight forward on enemy soil [S7].
- Siege: none early. Trebuchets in the Medieval wave.

**Exit [I + S22]**
- If the target defends with Horsemen or Composite Bowmen, or has walls, convert the plan into raiding:
  - pillage, steal workers, kill units for faith,
  - then take peace for tribute [S22].
- Re-strike with Pikemen + Crossbows + Trebuchets [S7].

## 6. Zulu (Shaka) — the classic "wait for the UU" timing civ

**Strength [I + S24]**
- Impi: replaces the Pikeman at **Civil Service** (Medieval), strength 16, throws spears before melee, needs no resource.
- Ikanda: replaces the Barracks at Bronze Working; gives melee units the Buffalo promotions.
- Trait: melee maintenance -50%, and 25% less XP needed for promotions [S24].
- Spike: Civil Service through roughly Rifling. An Impi can dominate Musketmen but loses to Riflemen and Gatling Guns [I].

**Type.** Infrastructure first, **but war starts early for XP**. glory7 (Deity, Standard) [S24]:
- "DoW early (before t60) ... Don't even try to get a city and just stack XP & kill his units."
- First real city at about T90-95 with 2-3 Range-promoted CBs.
- Upgrade Spearmen to Impi at Civil Service, "usually before t115".

**Opening (flexible) [S24][S25]**
- Builds (glory7): Scout, Monument, Scout, Granary, (Shrine), 3 Archers, Worker (or steal one), Pyramids, Archer, Settlers. Then Ikanda, Spearmen, CBs.
- Research: Pottery, Archery/Mining/Animal Husbandry, Masonry, luxury techs, Construction, Bronze Working, Horseback Riding, Writing, Currency, Machinery, Civil Service.
- Policy: Liberty for 3-4 cities [S24][S25]. Tradition with 2-3 cities also seen [S25].
- Before the war: farm XP on the target's units, or on a city-state with Archers (glory7) [S25].

**Strike [S24][S25]**
- Wave 1 (around T90-100): 6-10 CBs or Crossbows + 3-8 Spearmen/Impi.
  - glory7: "t92, 10 Xbows and 3 impis"; first city at T94, Siamese capital at T98.
  - hicks53081: "6 composites and 8 impi", Paris around T115.
- Siege: usually none; Impi plus ranged units. RedRover57 planned "a couple of cats" and regretted not building "more comp bows" [S25].
- Upgrades: Spearmen to Impi at Civil Service; CBs to Crossbows at Machinery.

**Exit [S25][I]**
- If only one city falls in 10+ turns (RedRover57 at about T115), add ranged units rather than more melee [S25].
- If an early worker steal is blocked by a Spearman, fall back to XP farming on a city-state [S25].

## 7. Mongolia (Genghis Khan) — medieval spike

**Strength [I + S26]**
- Keshik: replaces the Knight at **Chivalry**. Mounted ranged unit with 5 moves and faster XP; needs Horses.
- Khan: replaces the Great General and heals adjacent units.
- Trait: +30% vs city-states, mounted units +1 move.
- Spike: Chivalry through the Renaissance. Budweiser: "I don't think I can win it with just the Keshiks" [S26].

**Type.** A **late timed strike** with an early pre-build. Chariot Archers or Horsemen are built and promoted early, then upgraded at Chivalry.
- Deity Challenge #8 (BNW, Standard): Chivalry at about T92-105.
  - peddroelm had "14 chariots ready to upgrade".
  - fils "upgraded four chariot archers" [S26].
- An early grab is possible: hicks53081 took London and the Great Wall with chariots and horsemen around T97 [S26].

**Opening [S26][S27]**
- Builds (fils): Scout, Scout, Granary, Shrine, Caravan, Chariot Archer, Settler at size 4, Chariot Archer, Settler at size 5.
- Research (hicks53081): Pottery, Writing, Animal Husbandry, Mining, Archery, Trapping, The Wheel, Bronze Working, Philosophy, then Civil Service/Currency, then Chivalry.
- Policy: Honor (full) or Tradition then Honor [S26].
- Cities: 2-3 [S27].
- Before the war: Chariot Archers kill barbarians and stay alive for promotions. An early war on a weak neighbor is optional, to steal a settler or worker [S26].

**Strike [S26]**
- 8+ Keshiks + 2 melee for the capture; hicks53081 used "eight Keshik's and two horsemen".
- Upgrade every Chariot Archer on the turn Chivalry lands. Save gold for it [I]; in vanilla it cost 140 gold per Chariot Archer [S27].
- Siege: Keshiks + Khans; Trebuchets optional [I].

**Exit [S26][I]**
- Dense unit spam, rough terrain, or Pikes/Winged Hussars in numbers: retarget.
- Without Horses, delay the plan [I].

## 8. Assyria (Ashurbanipal)

**Strength [S28][I]**
- Siege Tower: replaces the Catapult at **Mathematics**. A melee siege unit whose aura gives adjacent units +50% vs the city; it cannot kill units by itself [S28].
- Royal Library: replaces the Library and gives XP to units built in the city.
- Trait: steal a tech on each city capture.
- Spike: Mathematics through Classical. beetle: "Siege towers don't keep their buff when upgraded" [S29].

**Type.** A **CB plus Siege Tower** timing.
- imMapex (BNW Deity): capture window "turns 80-110" [S28]. That is later than G1, because of a Temple of Artemis build and Royal Libraries.
- Tier-list critics say the BNW diplomatic hits are "insanely severe" [S8].

**Opening (imMapex) [S28]**
- Builds: Scout, Scout, Temple of Artemis, Shrine/Worker/Archer, Settler, Granary, CB, CB, Siege Tower, CB/Royal Library, Royal Library/Caravan, National College.
- Research: Archery, Mining, luxury, Construction, luxury, Mathematics, Writing, Philosophy.
- Policy: Tradition (Legalism, Landed Elite, Monarchy, Aristocracy, Oligarchy).
- Cities: 2 before the National College; a critic called that "quite low" [S28].

**Strike [S28][S29][I]**
- CBs + 1-2 Siege Towers + 1-2 melee (Spearmen/Warriors) to capture. Order: "Expo -> Capital or Capital -> Expo (Never Expo -> Expo)".
- claudiupb's failure: 3 Catapults + 2 Siege Towers lost against a capital defended by 2 Legions. "Maybe only Persia is doable with Siege Towers" [S29].
- Add CBs rather than siege when the defenders are strong melee units [I].

**Exit [S29][I]**
- Target too far away (Zedseayou: "early war will be futile" because of distance) [S29].
- Strong melee defenders (Legions, Hoplites).
- Fallback: a late war with Artillery (beetle) [S29].

## 9. Japan (Oda Nobunaga) — POOR SOURCES

**Strength [I]**
- Samurai: replaces the Longswordsman at **Steel** (Medieval). Needs Iron, generates Great Generals faster, can build Fishing Boats.
- Trait (Bushido): units fight at full strength when damaged. Players say it "doesn't change the strategy much" [S30].
- Zero: a late fighter.

**Type.** A **late timed strike** (Medieval).
- The only concrete source is JamesCivFan's Longswordsman rush: "Steel by turn 95-100", Tradition and Aristocracy, 3 cities, National College around T68-70, at least 8 Iron nearby, and 100 gold per Swordsman-to-Longswordsman upgrade [S3]. It says Japan is "better because of Samurai" [S3].
- Deity community game #36 (BNW) shows Japan players mostly not rushing; iron was split between Samurai and Frigates [S31].

**Opening [S3][I]**
- Research: luxury, then Bronze Working/Iron Working, then Writing to the National College, then Mathematics, Construction, Civil Service, Machinery, then Steel.
- Policy: Tradition (Aristocracy).
- Cities: 3.
- Before Steel: Swordsmen/Warriors and CBs defend and hunt barbarians. Pre-build Swordsmen to upgrade, and save 100 gold per unit [S3].

**Strike [S3][I]**
- 6-8 Samurai (upgraded Swordsmen) + 3-4 Crossbows or CBs + 1-2 Trebuchets if the target has walls.
- Declare right at Steel, around Standard T95-105.

**Exit [I].** Iron below the needed unit count, so delay. Great Wall or Castle with no siege. If Steel is late, after about Standard T120, drop to a defensive/economy plan.

## 10. Songhai (Askia)

**Strength [I + S32]**
- Mandekalu Cavalry: replaces the Knight at **Chivalry**, with no city-attack penalty; needs Horses.
- Trait: triple gold from barbarian camps and from captured cities [S33]. Embarked units defend themselves.
- Mud Pyramid Mosque: a temple replacement.
- Spike: Chivalry until Pikes and Lancers are common [I].

**Type.** A **"Chivalry slingshot" timed strike** [S32].
- Pre-build Horsemen; "When you reach chivalry, upgrade and attack" [S32 search summary].
- The vanilla-era thread aimed for Chivalry around T80-85 [S27]. Resipsa: "Civs that get early access to extra gold such as Songhai do this type of opening well" for the CB rush [S1].
- **Barbarian camps double as an economy plan here**, a strong pre-war role for early units [S33][I].

**Opening [S27][S33][I]**
- Builds (Redaxe): Scout, Warrior, Warrior, Scout, Warrior, Settler. Barbarian-camp gold bought a Worker [S33].
- Research: Animal Husbandry, Horseback Riding (Horsemen), Writing, Philosophy, Civil Service/Currency, then Chivalry [S27].
- Policy: Honor first (Military Tradition) before Chivalry [S32 search summary]. Or Liberty for the bulb [S27].
- Cities: 2 [S27].
- Before the war: Horsemen and Warriors clear barbarian camps for triple gold. That gold funds the upgrades [I].

**Strike [S27][S33][I]**
- 5-8 Mandekalu (upgraded Horsemen) + 2-3 Crossbows. Mandekalu can capture cities themselves.
- consentient won with "Mandekalu Cavalry and crossbow" against Morocco [S33].
- Upgrade the turn Chivalry lands. Siege is optional.

**Exit [S27][S33]**
- A walled target with Bowmen: snarzberry lost a game invading Babylon first [S27].
- A double DoW while the army is away (adcarrymaokai, budweiser) [S33]. AI rule [I]: keep 2 defenders at home.

## Source quality summary

**Good BNW sources:**
- Huns (the Attila Deity guide plus an Immortal Challenge thread).
- Zulu (glory7's guide plus Deity Challenge #1).
- Mongolia (Deity Challenge #8).
- Generic CB rush (three threads).

**Moderate:**
- Aztec (anecdotes and Deity Challenge #19, no full guide).
- Assyria (one unfinished guide plus one failed attack).
- Celts (BNW guide, but no build order; the best rush data is G&K).
- Greece (mostly vanilla or G&K opinions plus one BNW thread).

**Poor:**
- **Inca**: no rush source; the plan is inferred.
- **Japan**: only a generic Longswordsman rush and a non-rush community game.
- **Songhai**: the main warmonger thread is from 2011 (vanilla) despite its [BNW] tag; the BNW Deity Challenge thread has little opening detail.
