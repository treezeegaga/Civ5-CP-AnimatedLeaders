CP with Animated Leaders
========================

Community Patch 5.4.8 for multiplayer, plus animated 3D leaders when you talk to AI
civilizations in multiplayer games.

INSTALL
  1. Make sure Civilization V has ALL expansions and DLC installed
     (Steam > right-click Civilization V > Properties > DLC > tick everything).
  2. Unzip this whole folder anywhere.
  3. Double-click Install.bat.

PLAY
  - Start Civilization V normally. Do NOT enable mods in the Mods menu.
  - Multiplayer > Internet or LAN > host or join a NEW game.
  - Everyone should pick King difficulty. On King this pack gives the AI exactly the same
    rules as human players: no cheaper techs, policies, buildings, units or upgrades, no extra
    happiness, units, XP or worker speed, and it judges humans' armies like anyone else's.
    On other difficulties the AI gets Community Patch's usual bonuses.
  - Old saves from the unmodded game won't load while the pack is installed.
  - Everyone in the game needs the same pack version (shown by the installer).
  - Everyone must start the game with the same DirectX version. When Steam asks how to
    launch, all pick the DirectX 11 option (recommended). Mixing DirectX 9 and 11 in one game
    crashes it when the first turn ends.

UNINSTALL
  Double-click Uninstall.bat to go back to the normal game.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
